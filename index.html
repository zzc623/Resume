Biography

I am an Associate Professor and Chief Scientist at JancsiTech, where I lead the AI medical imaging R&D team. Previously, I was the Research Lead at ByteDance AI Healthcare Department and a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Stanford University, working with Prof. Lei Xing.

I obtained my Ph.D. degree from Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, supervised by Prof. Yaoqin Xie in June 2019. I received my B.S. from Sun Yat-Sen University in 2010, and was a visiting scholar at Virginia Tech from 01/2017 to 01/2018.

My research focuses on medical AI, including medical image reconstruction (CT/MRI), cancer prediction, and image analysis. I have published 85+ papers in top venues including Nature Communications, Lancet Digital Health, IEEE TMI, and MIA, with 2300+ citations.
